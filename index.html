Recipe List

1. Chocolate Cake

Steps:

Preheat oven to 425 F (218 C).

Melt butter and chocolate together.

Whisk eggs, yolks, and sugar until pale.

Fold chocolate and flour into egg mixture.

Bake for 12-14 minutes.

2. Veg Puffs

Steps:

Preheat oven to 400 F (200 C).

Saute onions, potatoes, peas, and spices.

Cut pastry sheets and fill with mixture.

Fold into triangles and seal edges.

Brush with milk and bake for 20-25 mins.

3. Cookies

Steps:

Whisk flour and baking powder together.

Beat butter and sugar until fluffy.

Mix in egg and chocolate, then dry ingredients.

Roll out, cut shapes, and bake at 350 F (175 C).
