Pen Shop — Types & Info

A demo page showing different pen types, descriptions and a simple filter. Use this for presentations or classroom demos.

Ballpoint Pen

Best for: Everyday writing, long-lasting ink.
Notes: Uses oil-based ink; low maintenance and inexpensive.

₹50 - ₹200

Gel Pen

Best for: Smooth writing and vibrant colors.
Notes: Water-based gel ink gives bolder lines; slightly slower drying.

₹70 - ₹350

Fountain Pen

Best for: Calligraphy, signatures, premium writing experiences.
Notes: Uses liquid ink & nib; requires occasional cleaning and proper ink.

₹400 - ₹6,000+

Rollerball Pen

Best for: Smooth writing with liquid ink but less bleed than fountain pens.
Notes: Uses water-based ink for fluid strokes.

₹120 - ₹900

Felt-tip / Marker

Best for: Drawing, coloring, posters, and bold lines.
Notes: Comes in fine/brush tips; permanent or washable variants.

₹80 - ₹500

Brush Pen

Best for: Calligraphy and brush-style lettering.
Notes: Flexible tip mimics brush strokes; popular with artists.

₹150 - ₹1,200

Stylus Pen

Best for: Tablets and smartphones — drawing or navigation.
Notes: Some styluses are active (pressure sensitive); others are simple conductive tips.

₹250 - ₹3,000

Highlighter

Best for: Emphasizing text in books and notes.
Notes: Available in fluorescent colors; some are low-bleed for thin paper.

₹30 - ₹250
